Games / 003In development

Shadow Warriors

A first-person dungeon game built around nine circles, tense combat and a tournament beneath a mountain.

  • Stamina-based attacks and directional blocking.
  • Equipment, upgrades and persistent progression.
Unity / C#Dungeon exploration
Inside the project

Explore a labyrinth, encounter human opponents and manage stamina during attacks and blocks. Equipment, upgrades and persistent progression connect the city and dungeon.

Health, stamina, currency, equipment and rewards persist between sessions. The city provides a place to prepare before returning to the dungeon.

Focus: Game systems, combat design, world building and visual production.

Games / 004In development

CyberShop

An ambient shop-management game set on a rain-soaked, neon-lit street. Manage stock, prices and the rhythm of a future city.

  • Stock, supplier orders, pricing and storage management.
  • Customer types, shop upgrades and daily accounting.
Unity / C#Management simulation
Inside the project

A fixed 2.5D storefront pairs everyday retail decisions with a living street. Customer types, supplier orders, storage, shop upgrades and daily accounting shape the simulation.

Paid incoming deliveries reserve storage capacity. Active assortment determines what customers can buy, while adjustable time controls let players manage the pace.

Focus: Economy design, progression, simulation systems and environmental presentation.
